First public release.

All your self-hosted services on one screen, each opening in its own tab that keeps the
session alive. Two addresses per machine picked by Wi-Fi network name, network scan,
import from Homer, Homarr and Heimdall, and sending ed2k, magnet and .torrent links to
your own aMule, qBittorrent, Transmission or SABnzbd.

Credentials are encrypted on the device. No accounts, no telemetry, no servers of ours.
